# Haemodialysis vascular access: evidence record (batch 27, not published)

**Lead guideline:** UK Kidney Association Clinical Practice Guideline on vascular access for haemodialysis. (2025), PMID 40813633, document id 2416. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text states: 1.1 We recommend focussed access advice for all adults and children anticipating or undergoing a period of haemodialysis, providing simple information outlining the relative merits of a range of access types [1C] 1.2 We recommend treating access choice as a patient decision, supported by the multidisciplinary team, allowing adequate consideration time, taking into account individual patient characteristics and priorities [1C] 1.3 We recommend advising fistula formation for adults and children with suitable anatomy and a likelihood of prolonged haemodialysis [1B] 1.4 We suggest advising catheter access for very small children, and when a short period on haemodialysis is anticipated [2C]
