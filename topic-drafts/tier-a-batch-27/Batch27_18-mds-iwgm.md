## All recommendations extracted from the stored text (8 statements)

### IWGM-MDS consensus proposals

1. It is recommended that (1) agranular or granular blast cells be defined (replacing the previous type I, II and III blasts), (2) dysplastic promyelocytes be distinguished from cytologically normal promyelocytes and from granular blast cells, (3) sufficient cells be counted to give a precise blast percentage, particularly at thresholds that are important for diagnosis or prognosis and (4) ring sideroblasts be defined as erythroblasts in which there are a minimum of 5 siderotic granules covering at least a third of the nuclear circumference.  
   *Anchor:* "It is recommended that (1) agranular or granular blast cells be defined (replacing the previous type I, II and III blasts), (2) dysplastic promyelocytes be distinguished from cytologically normal promyelocytes and from granular blast cells, (3) sufficient cells be counted to give a precise blast"
2. Recommendations should be applied in the context of the WHO classification.  
   *Anchor:* "Recommendations should be applied in the context of the WHO classification."
3. Proposals for minimal diagnostic criteria and for dealing with cases of possible MDS that do not meet these criteria ar e dealt with in an accompanying paper.9 Background: current myelodysplastic syndrome classification systems Several classification systems have been developed to predict survival or transition to AML following MDS diagnosis.  
   *Anchor:* "Proposals for minimal diagnostic criteria and for dealing with cases of possible MDS that do not meet these criteria ar e dealt with in an accompanying paper.9 Background: current myelodysplastic syndrome classification systems Several classification systems have been developed to predict survival"
4. After review- ing all the available bone marrow smears, the IWGM group recommended that myeloblasts in MDS should be classified as agranular or granular.  
   *Anchor:* "After review- ing all the available bone marrow smears, the IWGM group recommended that myeloblasts in MDS should be classified as agranular or granular."
5. These cells will thus include type II blasts as defined by FAB, as well as type III blasts as defined by Goasguen et al.12 Granular blasts must be distinguished from promye- locytes (see below).  
   *Anchor:* "These cells will thus include type II blasts as defined by FAB, as well as type III blasts as defined by Goasguen et al.12 Granular blasts must be distinguished from promye- locytes (see below)."
6. It should also be noted that when performing a mar- row dif ferential count, the myeloblast per centage should be determined by counting at least 500 nucleat- ed cells, with the total including at least 100 nonery- throid cells to improve precision.  
   *Anchor:* "It should also be noted that when performing a mar- row dif ferential count, the myeloblast per centage should be determined by counting at least 500 nucleat- ed cells, with the total including at least 100 nonery- throid cells to improve precision."
7. After a review of many cases of sideroblastic anemia, the group determined that ring sideroblasts should have at least 5 granules in a perinuclear distribution; that these granules could either surround the entire nucleus, be localized to portions of the perinuclear area or cover at least one third of the nucleus (Figures 3 and 4).  
   *Anchor:* "After a review of many cases of sideroblastic anemia, the group determined that ring sideroblasts should have at least 5 granules in a perinuclear distribution; that these granules could either surround the entire nucleus, be localized to portions of the perinuclear area or cover at least one third"

The gr oup recommends that when counting ring sideroblasts all stages of erythroid precursors be counted and should include at least 100 nucleated erythroid pre- cursors and for the definition of RARS the required num- ber of ring sideroblasts remains at 15% as defined previ- ously in the FAB and WHO classifications.  
   *Anchor:* "The gr oup recommends that when counting ring sideroblasts all stages of erythroid precursors be counted and should include at least 100 nucleated erythroid pre- cursors and for the definition of RARS the required num- ber of ring sideroblasts remains at 15% as defined previ- ously in the FAB and WHO classifications."
