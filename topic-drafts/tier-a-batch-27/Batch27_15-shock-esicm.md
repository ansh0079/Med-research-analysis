# Circulatory shock and haemodynamic monitoring: evidence record (batch 27, not published)

**Lead guideline:** Consensus on circulatory shock and hemodynamic monitoring. Task force of the European Society of Intensive Care Medicine. (2014), PMID 25392034, document id 2827. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text states: We recommend that shock be defined as a life-threatening, generalized maldistribution of blood flow resulting in failure to deliver and/or utilize adequate amounts of oxygen, leading to tissue dysoxia We recommend a target blood pressure during initial shock resuscitation of:–For uncontrolled hemorrhage due to trauma: MAP of 40 mmHg until bleeding is surgically controlled We recommend individualizing the target blood pressure during shock resuscitation We recommend to initially target a MAP of ≥65 mmHg
