## All recommendations of the lead guideline (11 statements + 0 body-text details)

### DM1 care consensus

1. Clinicians must monitor issues such as recurrent pneumonia at baseline and serially (±6 months), with pulmonary function tests, at least forced vital capacity (fvc).  
   *Anchor:* "clinicians must monitor issues such as recurrent pneumonia at baseline and serially (±6 months), with pulmonary function tests, at least forced vital capacity (fvc)."
2. Extreme care should be taken with analgesics and sedating anesthetic drugs (see mdf's practical suggestions for the anesthetic management of a myotonic dystrophy patient [myotonic.  
   *Anchor:* "extreme care should be taken with analgesics and sedating anesthetic drugs (see mdf's practical suggestions for the anesthetic management of a myotonic dystrophy patient [myotonic."
3. Should consult with a high-risk obstetrics and gynecology (obgyn) care provider before delivery and obtain ongoing antenatal care.  
   *Anchor:* "should consult with a high-risk obstetrics and gynecology (obgyn) care provider before delivery and obtain ongoing antenatal care."
4. Should be induced only at direction of obstetrician and after all necessary consultants assisting with the delivery are notified.  
   *Anchor:* "should be induced only at direction of obstetrician and after all necessary consultants assisting with the delivery are notified."
5. Should be referred to genetic counseling and family planning services if of child-bearing age.  
   *Anchor:* "should be referred to genetic counseling and family planning services if of child-bearing age."
6. Should receive parental counselling for prenatal genetic diagnosis or discussion of preimplantation genetic diagnosis.  
   *Anchor:* "should receive parental counselling for prenatal genetic diagnosis or discussion of preimplantation genetic diagnosis."
7. Intensive neonatal care is recommended for neonates that may have dm1  
   *Anchor:* "intensive neonatal care is recommended for neonates that may have dm1"
8. Access to a pediatric or neonatal specialist is recommended even if the fetus is known to be unaffected.  
   *Anchor:* "access to a pediatric or neonatal specialist is recommended even if the fetus is known to be unaffected."
9. Recommend annual eye examination, including slit-lamp eye examination.  
   *Anchor:* "recommend annual eye examination, including slit-lamp eye examination."
10. Physical examination should include abdominal palpation, including around gall bladder, and rectal examination for anal sphincter spasm and dyssynergic defecation for symptomatic patients.  
   *Anchor:* "physical examination should include abdominal palpation, including around gall bladder, and rectal examination for anal sphincter spasm and dyssynergic defecation for symptomatic patients."
11. Avoid oils-if the above fails, refer out for anal manometry  
   *Anchor:* "avoid oils-if the above fails, refer out for anal manometry"
