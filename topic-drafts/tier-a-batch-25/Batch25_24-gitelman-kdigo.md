# Hypokalaemia: evidence record (batch 25, not published)

**Lead guideline:** Gitelman syndrome: consensus and guidance from a Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes (KDIGO) Controversies Conference. (2017), PMID 28003083, document id 1666. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the KDIGO controversies-conference consensus on Gitelman syndrome. Directive sentences on diagnosis and management of that syndrome, including hypokalaemia in this condition, are listed. It is not a general hypokalaemia protocol.
