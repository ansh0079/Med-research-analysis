## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the 2024 Brazilian consensus on diagnosis and management of acute promyelocytic leukaemia. Directive sentences in that text are listed. The hypogranular or microgranular variant is described as paucity or absence of granules and bilobed nuclei, with leukocytosis in some cases. That description is included only where the sentence states it. Morphology-pitfall rules that are not in the text were not added.
