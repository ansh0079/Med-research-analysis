## Notes

The spreadsheet lead was document 7671 (PMID 28611889), a study of the LRINEC score for separating necrotizing fasciitis from cellulitis. Its directives are about that score and about urgent surgery for necrotizing fasciitis, not a cellulitis prescribing regimen. Document 8060 is the NICE prescribing summary. Document 5352 is the same text. Document 1515 is a point-of-care ultrasound diagnostic-accuracy paper. Doses are quoted only as stored, including footnote numbers that sit in the line. Lines shorter than 40 characters after normalisation, including the six-month prophylaxis review on its own, are omitted.
