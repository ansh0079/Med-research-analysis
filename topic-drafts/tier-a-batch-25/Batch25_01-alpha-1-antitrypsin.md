## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the 2018 Portuguese consensus on the management of alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency. The list is the directive sentences found in that text. A 2024 EARCO Delphi on monitoring lung disease (document 2205) is narrower and was not substituted. The 2003 ATS/ERS statement is older.
