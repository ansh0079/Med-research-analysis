# Antibiotic stewardship for common infections: evidence record (batch 26, not published)

**Lead guideline:** Implementing an Antibiotic Stewardship Program: Guidelines by the Infectious Diseases Society of America and the Society for Healthcare Epidemiology of America. (2016), PMID 27080992, document id 1647. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the IDSA and SHEA guideline on implementing an antibiotic stewardship programme. It recommends preauthorisation and/or prospective audit and feedback, suggests against relying only on didactic materials, and suggests facility-specific clinical practice guidelines with a plan to spread and implement them. The file lists the "we recommend" and "we suggest" sentences.
