# Pegloticase and lesinurad in refractory gout: indications, infusion reactions: evidence record (batch 26, not published)

**Lead guideline:** 2020 American College of Rheumatology Guideline for the Management of Gout (2020), PMID 32390306, document id 5443. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the 2020 American College of Rheumatology guideline for the management of gout. It recommends a treat-to-target strategy and includes conditional recommendations on when to start urate-lowering therapy. Pegloticase and lesinurad both appear in the stored text. The spreadsheet lead was the 2016 EULAR update; this newer gout guideline was used instead. Statements are the "we recommend" and "we conditionally recommend" sentences.
