# management of PRESS: evidence record (batch 26, not published)

**Lead guideline:** Posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome. (2017), PMID 28054130, document id 2848. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is a review of posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome. It says a reduction of blood pressure by 25% from baseline is recommended, that blood-pressure fluctuations should be avoided, that the trigger or underlying disease should be managed early, and that hypomagnesaemia should be avoided with serum magnesium kept in the high range. No separate PRES treatment guideline was in the database.

## All recommendations of the lead guideline (5 statements + 0 body-text details)

### PRES review

1. Epidemiological data in particular should be interpreted with caution, since the syndrome may still be significantly underdiagnosed as the condition can be hard to confirm.  
   *Anchor:* "Epidemiological data in particular should be interpreted with caution, since the syndrome may still be significantly underdiagnosed as the condition can be hard to confirm."
2. A reduction of blood pressure levels by 25% from baseline values is recommended.  
   *Anchor:* "A reduction of blood pressure levels by 25% from baseline values is recommended."
3. As with other conditions, blood pressure fluctuations should be avoided and the continuous administration of antihypertensive drugs under hemodynamic monitoring should be considered [50].  
   *Anchor:* "As with other conditions, blood pressure fluctuations should be avoided and the continuous administration of antihypertensive drugs under hemodynamic monitoring should be considered [50]."
4. Whenever possible, the elimination of the triggering factor or management of the underlying pathology should be initiated early during the course of the disease [1, 13, 15].  
   *Anchor:* "Whenever possible, the elimination of the triggering factor or management of the underlying pathology should be initiated early during the course of the disease [1, 13, 15]."
5. Due to the fact that magnesium levels are reduced in a high number of patients with PRES, coupled to its known prophylactic anticonvulsive and vasodilating effects, hypomagnesemia should be avoided and serum levels be maintained in the high normal range [15, 36].  
   *Anchor:* "Due to the fact that magnesium levels are reduced in a high number of patients with PRES, coupled to its known prophylactic anticonvulsive and vasodilating effects, hypomagnesemia should be avoided and serum levels be maintained in the high normal range [15, 36]."
