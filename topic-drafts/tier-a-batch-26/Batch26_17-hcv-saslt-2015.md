# hepatitis c treatment: evidence record (batch 26, not published)

**Lead guideline:** SASLT position statement on the direct-acting antiviral agents for the treatment of hepatitis C virus infection (2015), PMID 25843190, document id 6349. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The spreadsheet lead is a 298-word abstract on drug-drug interactions with direct-acting antivirals and contains no should or recommend sentence. This file uses the 2015 Saudi Association for the Study of Liver Diseases and Transplantation position on direct-acting antivirals. It says treatment should be prioritised for Metavir F3–F4 and extrahepatic disease such as HCV immune-complex nephropathy, that the new agents or combinations are generally pangenotypic, that therapy should be prioritised for those in most need, that intensive measures must identify and treat those patients, and that regimens should be individualised.
