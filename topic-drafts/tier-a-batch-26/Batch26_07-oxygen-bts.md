# Oxygen therapy targets in COPD: evidence record (batch 26, not published)

**Lead guideline:** BTS guideline for oxygen use in adults in healthcare and emergency settings (2017), PMID 28507176, document id 6491. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the British Thoracic Society guideline on oxygen use in adults in healthcare and emergency settings. It says oxygen should be prescribed to a target saturation of 94–98% for most acutely ill patients, or 88–92% or a patient-specific range for people at risk of hypercapnic respiratory failure. The good-practice points marked in the stored text are listed, including taking a history when possible, not stopping oxygen to measure room-air oximetry when oxygen is clearly required, and adjusting track-and-trigger systems for a lower target in hypercapnic risk.
