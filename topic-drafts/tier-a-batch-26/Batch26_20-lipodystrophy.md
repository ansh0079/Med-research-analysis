# Lipodystrophy syndromes: partial vs generalised, metabolic complications, leptin replacement: evidence record (batch 26, not published)

**Lead guideline:** The Diagnosis and Management of Lipodystrophy Syndromes: A Multi-Society Practice Guideline. (2016), PMID 27710244, document id 2846. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the multi-society practice guideline on diagnosis and management of lipodystrophy syndromes. Directive sentences are listed, including those on generalised and partial disease and on metabolic complications where the text states them. A 2022 metreleptin review is shorter and was not substituted.

   *Anchor:* "Cardiac disease Blood pressure should be measured at least annually."

23. (Class IIa, Level C) Evaluation for ischemia and rhythm monitoring should be considered in patients with progeroid disorders and FPLD2 with cardiomyopathy.  
   *Anchor:* "(Class IIa, Level C) Evaluation for ischemia and rhythm monitoring should be considered in patients with progeroid disorders and FPLD2 with cardiomyopathy."
24. Kidney disease Urine protein should be measured annually using 24-hour urine collection or spot urine protein-to-creatinine ratio.  
   *Anchor:* "Kidney disease Urine protein should be measured annually using 24-hour urine collection or spot urine protein-to-creatinine ratio."
25. Kidney biopsy should be performed as clinically indicated, and pathology may include diabetic nephropathy, focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (especially in CGL) (34) or MPGN (especially in APL) (17).  
   *Anchor:* "Kidney biopsy should be performed as clinically indicated, and pathology may include diabetic nephropathy, focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (especially in CGL) (34) or MPGN (especially in APL) (17)."
26. Clinicians should consider screening for brain tumors in children who present with idiopathic AGL or atypical CGL.  
   *Anchor:* "Clinicians should consider screening for brain tumors in children who present with idiopathic AGL or atypical CGL."

   *Anchor:* "Diet Most patients should follow diets with balanced macronutrient composition."

   *Anchor:* "(Class I, Level C) Very-low-fat diets should be used in chylomicronemia-induced acute pancreatitis."
29. (Class I, Level C) A dietician should be consulted for specialized dietary needs, especially in infants and young children.Overfeeding should be avoided.  
   *Anchor:* "(Class I, Level C) A dietician should be consulted for specialized dietary needs, especially in infants and young children.Overfeeding should be avoided."
30. Food restriction to control metabolic complications must be balanced by requirements for growth in children.  
   *Anchor:* "Food restriction to control metabolic complications must be balanced by requirements for growth in children."
31. Patients should follow a 50–60% carbohydrate, 20–30% fat, and approximately 20% protein diet.  
   *Anchor:* "Patients should follow a 50–60% carbohydrate, 20–30% fat, and approximately 20% protein diet."
32. Simple sugars should be restricted in preference for high-fiber complex carbohydrates, distributed evenly among meals and snacks and consumed in combination with protein or fat.  
   *Anchor:* "Simple sugars should be restricted in preference for high-fiber complex carbohydrates, distributed evenly among meals and snacks and consumed in combination with protein or fat."
33. Dietary fat should be primarily cis-mono-unsaturated fats and long-chain omega-3 fatty acids.  
   *Anchor:* "Dietary fat should be primarily cis-mono-unsaturated fats and long-chain omega-3 fatty acids."
34. During acute pancreatitis, bowel rest followed by a very-low-fat (<20 g) diet should be used.  
   *Anchor:* "During acute pancreatitis, bowel rest followed by a very-low-fat (<20 g) diet should be used."
35. Exercise Patients with lipodystrophy should be encouraged to exercise in the absence of specific contraindications.  
   *Anchor:* "Exercise Patients with lipodystrophy should be encouraged to exercise in the absence of specific contraindications."
36. (Class IIa, Level C) Patients with subtypes of lipodystrophy predisposed to cardiomyopathy should undergo cardiac evaluation before initiating an exercise regimen.  
   *Anchor:* "(Class IIa, Level C) Patients with subtypes of lipodystrophy predisposed to cardiomyopathy should undergo cardiac evaluation before initiating an exercise regimen."
37. Most patients should be encouraged to be physically active.  
   *Anchor:* "Most patients should be encouraged to be physically active."

   *Anchor:* "However, strenuous exercise should be avoided in patients with cardiomyopathy."

   *Anchor:* "To reduce the risk of hypoglycemia, frequent glucose monitoring is recommended."

   *Anchor:* "(Class 1, Level C) Fibrates and/or long-chain omega-3 fatty acids should be used for triglycerides >500 mg/dL and may be considered for triglycerides >200 mg/dL."

   *Anchor:* "(Class IIb, Level C) Lipids should be managed in accordance with U.S."

54. Cosmetic treatment Patients should be assessed for distress related to lipodystrophy and referred as necessary to mental health professionals and/or plastic surgeons.  
   *Anchor:* "Cosmetic treatment Patients should be assessed for distress related to lipodystrophy and referred as necessary to mental health professionals and/or plastic surgeons."
55. (Class IIa, Level C) If contraception is needed, progestin-only or nonhormonal contraceptives should be considered.  
   *Anchor:* "(Class IIa, Level C) If contraception is needed, progestin-only or nonhormonal contraceptives should be considered."
56. (Class IIa, Level C) If estrogen replacement is needed, transdermal estrogen should be used.  
   *Anchor:* "(Class IIa, Level C) If estrogen replacement is needed, transdermal estrogen should be used."
57. Pregnancy Pregnant patients should receive prenatal care from an obstetrician experienced in managing diabetes and a physician experienced in managing lipodystrophy.

(Class IIa, level C) Should a patient become pregnant while taking metreleptin, clinicians may consider continuing metreleptin if withdrawal would harm the mother and fetus and the patient understands that the effects of metreleptin in pregnancy are unknown (FDA category C), and wishes to continue.
