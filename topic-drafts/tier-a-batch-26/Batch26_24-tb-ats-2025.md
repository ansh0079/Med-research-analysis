# TB treatment: RIPE regimen, hepatotoxicity monitoring, directly observed therapy, drug-resistant TB: evidence record (batch 26, not published)

**Lead guideline:** Updates on the Treatment of Drug-Susceptible and Drug-Resistant Tuberculosis: An Official ATS/CDC/ERS/IDSA Clinical Practice Guideline. (2025), PMID 40693952, document id 2403. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the official ATS, CDC, ERS and IDSA update on treatment of drug-susceptible and drug-resistant tuberculosis. It recommends a 4-month regimen of 2HRZ(E)/2HR rather than the 6-month 2HRZ(E)/4HR regimen in the population that sentence names, and 6-month BPaL or BPaLM regimens rather than regimens longer than 15 months for the drug-resistant populations named in those sentences. The spreadsheet lead was the 2016 drug-susceptible TB guideline.
