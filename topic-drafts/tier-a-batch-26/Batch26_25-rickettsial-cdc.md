## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the CDC recommendations and reports on diagnosis and management of tickborne rickettsial diseases, including Rocky Mountain spotted fever, other spotted-fever group infections, ehrlichiosis and anaplasmosis. The objectives in the stored text include that doxycycline is the treatment of choice for suspected disease in adults and children and that early empiric antibacterial therapy can prevent severe disease and death.
