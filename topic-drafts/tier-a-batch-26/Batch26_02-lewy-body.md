# Lewy body dementia diagnosis and symptomatic therapy: evidence record (batch 26, not published)

**Lead guideline:** Diagnosis and management of dementia with Lewy bodies: Fourth consensus report of the DLB Consortium. (2017), PMID 28592453, document id 1679. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the fourth DLB Consortium consensus report. Probable DLB should be diagnosed when one or more indicative biomarkers is found together with one or more core clinical features, and should not be diagnosed from biomarkers alone. The file lists the directive sentences in the stored prose. A 2026 Delphi on dopaminergic imaging and cardiac MIBG is narrower and was not substituted.
