# Osteomalacia and Rickets: evidence record (batch 26, not published)

**Lead guideline:** Nutritional Rickets and Osteomalacia in the Twenty-first Century: Revised Concepts, Public Health, and Prevention Strategies (2017), PMID 28612338, document id 7708. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is a review of nutritional rickets and osteomalacia. It states that children with nutritional rickets should be treated with vitamin D for at least 3 months, with daily doses given by age band, plus calcium of at least 500 mg/day, followed by lifelong vitamin D because the underlying risk does not change. It also states that authorities should identify risk groups, that supplementation of high-risk people should be part of immigrant and refugee assessment, that monitored vitamin D should be in antenatal care, and that fortification should not be limited to dairy foods.
