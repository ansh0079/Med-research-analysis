## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the 2016 IDSA and ATS guideline on hospital-acquired and ventilator-associated pneumonia in adults. The "we recommend" and "we suggest" sentences are listed, including use of antibiogram data, noninvasive sampling with semiquantitative cultures to diagnose ventilator-associated pneumonia, and withholding rather than continuing antibiotics in the setting that sentence names.
