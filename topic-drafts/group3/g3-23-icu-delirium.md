# ICU delirium: evidence record (group 3, not published)

**Status:** draft record, not published. No production load was run.

**Lead document:** Delirium: prevention, diagnosis and management (NICE CG103). Stored document id 9328. PMID none stored. Database year 2010. Evidence tier stored as guideline.

**Topics this record serves:**
- ICU delirium: CAM-ICU assessment, prevention, and management

**Scope:** NICE CG103 statements on assessment, prevention and treatment that match ICU delirium, including the 2023 wording that critical care uses CAM-ICU or ICDSC rather than the 4AT. The guideline also covers hospital wards and long-term care; those settings are not the focus of the questions. Document 9330, the PADIS guideline, was read and was not required once CG103 stated the CAM-ICU rule.

## Synopsis

The stored NICE text says that if delirium indicators are present, a competent practitioner uses the 4AT, but in critical care or the recovery room after surgery the CAM-ICU or the Intensive Care Delirium Screening Checklist is used instead. People at risk are not moved between wards or rooms unless necessary, and a tailored multicomponent prevention package is delivered by a trained multidisciplinary team. At least daily observation looks for new fluctuations. The possible cause is identified and managed. If distress or risk remains after de-escalation, short-term haloperidol, usually for one week or less, may be considered, starting at the lowest clinically appropriate dose.
