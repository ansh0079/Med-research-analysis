# IBD in pregnancy: evidence record (group 3, not published)

**Status:** draft record, not published. No production load was run.

**Lead document:** British Society of Gastroenterology consensus guidelines on the management of inflammatory bowel disease in adults. Stored document id 2387. PMID 31562236. Database year 2019. Evidence tier stored as guideline.

**Topics this record serves:**
- IBD in Pregnancy

**Scope:** Pregnancy and breastfeeding statements in the stored 2019 BSG adult IBD guideline. The rest of that long guideline is not listed. Document 2884 is an IBD COVID document and does not discuss pregnancy, so it was not used.

## Synopsis

The stored guideline says methotrexate is teratogenic and should not be given to women of childbearing age without assured contraception during treatment and for 6 months after stopping. If adherence to contraception is uncertain, alternative therapy should be used. Pregnancy on methotrexate leads to stopping the drug and high-dose folic acid, 15 mg daily, for at least 6 weeks. The text suggests men may not need to stop methotrexate before conception. Anti-TNF therapy is counselled through pregnancy, with continuation favoured in active or high-risk disease and a possible stop at the start of the third trimester if disease is inactive and the patient wishes to stop. Delivery follows obstetric reasons except active perianal disease or a pouch, where caesarean is often preferred, and medicines that are safe in pregnancy continue in breastfeeding.
