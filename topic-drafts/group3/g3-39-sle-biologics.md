- Systemic lupus erythematosus biologic therapy

**Scope:** Biologic and related immunosuppressive sentences in the stored 2023 EULAR SLE update. Hydroxychloroquine dosing and the rest of the guideline are not listed. Missing spaces in the stored text, such as nephrit is and facilita te, are kept.

## Synopsis

The stored update says prompt immunosuppressants, naming methotrexate, azathioprine and mycophenolate, and/or anifrolumab or belimumab, should be considered to control disease and taper glucocorticoids. Cyclophosphamide and rituximab are considered for organ-threatening and refractory disease respectively. For active lupus nephritis, glucocorticoids with mycophenolate or low-dose intravenous cyclophosphamide are the anchor drugs, and belimumab or a calcineurin inhibitor should be considered as add-on therapy. A separate sentence grades belimumab and anifrolumab 1a/A when they should be considered. Prior conventional immunosuppression should not be mandatory before anifrolumab or belimumab, and refractory cases may consider rituximab graded 2b/C.
