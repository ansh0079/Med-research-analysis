## Synopsis

For low global severity, the stored review prefers topical treatment alone so that systemic immunosuppression can be avoided. Vitamin D supplementation should be considered, and restrictive clothing at affected sites is to be avoided. Topical tacrolimus on a large area needs caution because of systemic absorption. Sclerotic skin is recommended for longer-wave UVA, and non-sclerotic or superficial sclerotic disease for narrowband UVB. Patients are told about secondary skin cancers, and suspicious lesions should be biopsied. Mouth lichenoid change is treated with a steroid rinse, followed by 20 to 30 minutes without food or fluid, and preservative-free eye formulations are preferred.
