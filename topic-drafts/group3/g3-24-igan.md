## Synopsis

The stored perspective says a kidney biopsy is recommended when the picture fits IgA nephropathy and proteinuria exceeds 0.5 g/day or eGFR is decreased. Isolated microhaematuria with proteinuria below 0.5 g/day and a normal eGFR is generally not an indication. Nefecon and sparsentan are described as approved in Europe only after a biopsy and above a proteinuria threshold, so a liberal biopsy policy is advised around that threshold. MEST-C is for prognosis, not for choosing a regimen. Supportive CKD therapy should now include an SGLT2 inhibitor. Reduced-dose corticosteroids remain considered, but their role in European populations should be judged critically, and prophylaxis including against Pneumocystis is recommended when they are used.
