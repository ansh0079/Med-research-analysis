**Status:** draft record, not published. No production load was run.

**Lead document:** Guideline for the diagnosis and treatment of Faecal Incontinence-A UEG/ESCP/ESNM/ESPCG collaboration. Stored document id 1923. PMID 35303758. Database year 2022. Evidence tier stored as guideline.

**Topics this record serves:**
- Anorectal Disorders

**Scope:** The assigned document is a faecal-incontinence guideline, not a guideline for every anorectal disorder. Haemorrhoids, fissure and fistula are not covered here. Quotes are diagnosis and first-assessment statements, plus one device restriction.

## Synopsis

The stored guideline says the first consultation should establish the exact complaint, and diagnosis should start with the history. Other causes of diarrhoea, including colorectal cancer, inflammatory bowel disease, coeliac disease, diabetes and prolapse, should be ruled out. Anorectal inspection and examination look for other pathology, previous surgery or trauma, impaction, and anal tone. Digital rectal examination is performed in a position that is reliable and comfortable. Predisposing factors and the psychological and social impact are considered, and expectations are discussed. The Procon/ProTect device should not be used when stool is continuously liquid.
