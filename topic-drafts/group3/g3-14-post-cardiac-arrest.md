The stored temperature is printed as 37 .5 °C. Document 1846 was not used here: its body is the 2021 ERC/ESICM post-resuscitation text, not special-circumstances arrest.

## Synopsis

The stored 2025 AHA text recommends 100 percent inspired oxygen after ROSC until oxygen saturation or PaO2 can be measured, and then says hypoxaemia should be avoided. Hypotension is avoided by keeping mean arterial pressure at least 65 mm Hg. A protocolised temperature strategy is recommended for adults who do not follow verbal commands, keeping temperature between 32 °C and 37 .5 °C as printed, and routine rapid infusion of cold fluid for prehospital cooling is not recommended. Emergency coronary angiography is for suspected cardiac arrest with persistent ST elevation, including if the patient is in coma. Clinically apparent seizures are treated, and the text recommends prompt EEG when the patient does not follow commands.
