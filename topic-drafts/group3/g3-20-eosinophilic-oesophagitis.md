**Scope:** Diagnosis and selected treatment statements from the BSG and paediatric gastroenterology guideline, which includes children and adults. ## Synopsis

The stored guideline says eosinophilic oesophagitis should be diagnosed when there are relevant oesophageal symptoms and a peak eosinophil count of at least 15 per 0.3 mm squared, after other causes of oesophageal eosinophilia are considered. It should be strongly considered in adults with dysphagia or food bolus obstruction, and considered in children of any age with age-typical symptoms. Azathioprine and 6-mercaptopurine are not recommended, and nor are sodium cromoglycate, montelukast and antihistamines for the oesophagitis itself. A dietitian is strongly recommended for elimination and food reintroduction. Allergy testing is not recommended for choosing the diet. After topical steroids are stopped, relapse is described as high and maintenance treatment should be recommended after clinical review.
