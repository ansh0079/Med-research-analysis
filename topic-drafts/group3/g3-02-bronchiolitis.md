**Topics this record serves:**
- Bronchiolitis and viral wheeze

**Scope:** Infants under 12 months in the 2025 Australasian bronchiolitis guideline. The stored tier is literature. The guideline itself says evidence for infants aged 12–24 months should be interpreted with greater diagnostic uncertainty, and that age band is outside this record.

## Synopsis

The 2025 Australasian update says not to use beta2 agonists in infants under 12 months who present to hospital or are hospitalised with bronchiolitis, and not to use adrenaline or epinephrine in infants in that setting. It also says not to use systemic or local glucocorticoids in infants with bronchiolitis. Supplemental oxygen is recommended if saturation is persistently under 90 percent in infants aged at least 6 weeks. It is also recommended if saturation is persistently under 92 percent in infants under 6 weeks, or under 12 months with an underlying health condition, and oxygen is stopped once saturation is 92 percent or greater. Infants who cannot maintain oral hydration should receive supplemental hydration, with the nasogastric route preferred first in moderate bronchiolitis.
