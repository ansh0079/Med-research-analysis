**Topics this record serves:**
- Nephrolithiasis: Calcium Stones
- Recurrent nephrolithiasis medical prevention

**Scope:** Diagnosis and medical prevention statements in the 2016 calcium nephrolithiasis consensus. Document 6860 is a lemon-juice trial in recurrent calcium oxalate stones and was not used as the guideline.

## Synopsis

The stored consensus says patients should take an age- and sex-appropriate amount of dietary calcium and should be 25-hydroxyvitamin D replete. Stone analysis should be done, and repeated because composition can change. Thiazides are offered for high or relatively high urine calcium with recurrent calcium stones, and they are given with potassium. Sodium citrate is avoided because sodium increases calcium excretion. People with hypocitraturia are told to reduce non-dairy animal protein. Incomplete distal renal tubular acidosis is suspected when unexplained recurrent stones are mostly or only hydroxyapatite, and a first brushite or apatite stone is investigated for a systemic cause.
