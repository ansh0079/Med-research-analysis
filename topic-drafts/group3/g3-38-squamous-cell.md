## Synopsis

The stored text says common primary cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma shall be classified as high-risk or low-risk, and advanced disease as locally advanced, locoregional or distant. Photographic documentation is strongly recommended, and clinical plus dermatoscopic photographs are recommended before biopsy. Histology is the gold standard, so biopsy or excision with histological confirmation is performed for every clinically suspected lesion, and a histopathological diagnosis shall be made if invasive disease is suspected. The longest clinical diameter, including the rim of erythema, is recorded before surgery. The pathology report shall include specimen type, thickness or depth, grade, perineural invasion, desmoplastic type and margins.
