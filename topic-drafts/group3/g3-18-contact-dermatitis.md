## Synopsis

The stored update says patch testing should be postponed in severe or generalised active dermatitis, and patients should be told the purpose, the method and the symptoms that may occur. History and examination should guide which materials are tested. Commercial allergens should be of pharmaceutical quality, and materials should be stored at 4 °C, protected from light. The dosing of liquids by use of a micropipette is strongly recommended. A day-2 reading as the only reading is not appropriate. A positive reaction to a patient's own product should raise the possibility of a false-positive result.
