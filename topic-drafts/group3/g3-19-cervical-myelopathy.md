**Scope:** The 2017 guideline's surgical and non-operative recommendations for mild, moderate and severe degenerative cervical myelopathy, and for non-myelopathic patients with cord compression. Quality and strength labels are included when they sit in the same stored sentence.

## Synopsis

The stored guideline recommends surgery for severe degenerative cervical myelopathy, with moderate-quality evidence and a strong recommendation, and it also recommends surgery for moderate disease. For mild disease it suggests surgery or a supervised trial of structured rehabilitation, and it recommends operation if non-operative care is followed by neurological deterioration. Prophylactic surgery is suggested against when there is cord compression without myelopathy or radiculopathy, and those patients are counselled and followed. Patients who already have radiculopathy are described as higher risk and may be offered surgery or close follow-up or structured rehabilitation.
