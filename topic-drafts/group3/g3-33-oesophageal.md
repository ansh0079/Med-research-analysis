## Synopsis

When staging imaging suggests central airway involvement, the stored text says bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound should generally be done. FDG-PET can be carried out in candidates for oesophagectomy. Supra-carinal tumours are recommended for a McKeown procedure. Endoscopic resection is described as curative for T1a Barrett's adenocarcinoma with well to moderate differentiation and no lymphovascular invasion, and surgery is recommended for the other T1 cases in that passage. Locally advanced squamous carcinoma is treated with the CROSS chemoradiotherapy regimen followed by surgery, including after a complete clinical response if the tumour is still resectable. Definitive chemoradiotherapy is the preferred option when squamous carcinoma is unresectable or the patient cannot or will not have surgery.
