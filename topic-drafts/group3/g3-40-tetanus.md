# Tetanus prophylaxis in major trauma: evidence record (group 3, not published)

**Status:** draft record, not published. No production load was run.

**Lead document:** Chinese expert consensus on tetanus prophylaxis in major trauma (2026 Edition) Stored document id 6903. PMID 42637638. Database year 2026. Evidence tier stored as guideline.

**Topics this record serves:**
- Tetanus: immunisation status, wound assessment, tetanus immunoglobulin, metronidazole

**Scope:** Major-trauma tetanus prophylaxis in the stored Chinese expert consensus. It is not a general minor-wound guideline. Passive immunisation is quoted as the stored dual strategy, not as a separate dose table. Debridement is limited to the first principle of heavily contaminated open wounds.

## Synopsis

When the immunisation history cannot be confirmed quickly, the stored consensus manages the patient as incompletely immunised and uses active plus passive immunisation, without delaying resuscitation. An incomplete or uncertain series should start a complete tetanus toxoid course as early as possible. A booster is given if a completed series is more than 5 years old in a tetanus-prone wound, and if more than 10 years have passed a booster is given regardless of wound type. Vaccination is not withheld because of post-traumatic immunosuppression, and prophylaxis proceeds in parallel with resuscitation. Metronidazole does not replace immunoprophylaxis, and initial debridement of heavily contaminated open wounds removes visible foreign material and obvious necrosis rather than attempting one-stage complete debridement.
