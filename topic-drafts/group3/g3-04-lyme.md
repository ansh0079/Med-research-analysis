**Status:** draft record, not published. No production load was run.

**Lead document:** Evidence assessments and guideline recommendations in Lyme disease: the clinical management of known tick bites, erythema migrans rashes and persistent disease (ILADS). Stored document id 6953. PMID none stored. Database year 2014. Evidence tier stored as guideline.

**Topics this record serves:**
- Lyme disease: Borrelia, tick bite, erythema migrans, disseminated disease, doxycycline

**Scope:** The stored text is the 2014 ILADS guideline on tick-bite prophylaxis, erythema migrans treatment, and antibiotic retreatment. Grades in the text are very low-quality evidence. This is not an IDSA guideline. Disseminated disease is addressed only where the stored retreatment and persistent-manifestation sentences speak to it.

## Synopsis

The stored ILADS text recommends against a single 200 mg dose of doxycycline for Lyme disease prophylaxis, and it names 100–200 mg of doxycycline twice daily for 20 days as the preferred prophylaxis regimen. For erythema migrans it says regimens of 20 or fewer days of penicillin, amoxicillin, cefuroxime or doxycycline, and 10 or fewer days of azithromycin, are not recommended. Initial therapy in the stored text is 4–6 weeks of amoxicillin, cefuroxime or doxycycline, or at least 21 days of azithromycin, with amoxicillin, cefuroxime or doxycycline named as first-line agents. Retreatment, when undertaken, is described as another 4–6 weeks, and the original diagnosis is reassessed before that retreatment. Patients are told to report tick-borne manifestations, including those suggestive of Clostridioides difficile infection.
