## Synopsis

The stored consensus says primary PCI of the infarct-related artery is indicated in STEMI. Radial access and drug-eluting stents rather than bare-metal stents are described as a Class I indication. Unfractionated heparin is a class I recommendation during primary PCI, and anticoagulation is recommended for all patients in addition to antiplatelet therapy. Direct stenting is recommended if possible, and recent-generation drug-eluting stents are recommended over bare-metal stents. There is no evidence in the text for routine glycoprotein IIb/IIIa inhibitors, and routine thrombus aspiration is described as downgraded from IIa to III, not recommended.
