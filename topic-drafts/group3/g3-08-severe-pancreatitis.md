## Synopsis

The stored WSES text says patients with organ failure should be admitted to an intensive care unit when possible. It lists Ranson criteria and APACHE II among scores that were assessed on admission or within 48 hours. On admission, ultrasound is used to look for a biliary cause, and triglyceride and calcium are measured when gallstones and a significant alcohol history are absent. Infected necrosis is treated with antibiotics that penetrate pancreatic necrosis, routine antifungal prophylaxis is not recommended, and no specific drug treatment other than organ support and nutrition is advised. Enteral nutrition is recommended, invasive ventilation should use lung-protective strategies, and the text says clinicians should be cautious not to over-resuscitate early severe pancreatitis.
