## Synopsis

The stored AASLD guidance says patients with NAFLD should be screened for type 2 diabetes. Initial evaluation includes metabolic comorbidities, alcohol intake, other liver diseases, and examination for signs of insulin resistance. Primary risk assessment aims to find people unlikely to have advanced fibrosis, using a FIB-4 under 1.3 as the low-risk example, and the text calls FIB-4 the most validated of the simple scores. Weight loss of 3 to 5 percent improves steatosis, while greater than 10 percent is generally required to improve NASH and fibrosis. Liver biopsy is considered when the diagnosis is uncertain. Risks of long-term high-dose vitamin E, for example 800 IU daily, should be discussed before it is started.
