**Topics this record serves:**

**Scope:** Imaging, biochemical work-up and follow-up statements in the stored ESE adrenal incidentaloma guideline. The database year is 2018; the stored text is the Fassnacht 2023 guideline. Document 1272 is a systematic review of non-functioning incidentalomas and was not the source. Medical therapy of Cushing syndrome is a separate topic and is not built from this file.

## Synopsis

The stored ESE text says that a noncontrast CT consistent with a benign mass, homogeneous and 10 Hounsfield units or less, needs no further imaging. Every patient is assessed for hormone excess, including a 1-mg overnight dexamethasone test, and a post-dexamethasone cortisol above 50 nmol/L without overt Cushing signs is called mild autonomous cortisol secretion without further stratification. Pheochromocytoma is excluded with plasma free or urinary fractionated metanephrines when imaging is not typical of a benign adenoma. Biopsy is recommended against unless there is extra-adrenal malignancy. Surgery is recommended against for an asymptomatic non-functioning unilateral mass with obvious benign imaging, and further imaging is recommended against when follow-up imaging is already clearly benign.
