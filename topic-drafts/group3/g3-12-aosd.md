## Synopsis

The stored review describes spiking fever above 39–40 °C, a pink–salmon transient rash, and arthralgia or polyarthritis. Ferritin and C-reactive protein, with transaminases, are described as commonly raised. Diagnosis is clinical, after excluding mimics, and the text says there are no diagnostic criteria even though Yamaguchi and Fautrel criteria are the most used. First-line therapy is described as NSAIDs and glucocorticoids. IL-1 inhibitors named are anakinra, canakinumab and rilonacept. Canakinumab is described as a fully human IL-1β antibody, EMA-approved for sJIA/AOSD at 150 mg or 300 mg every 4 weeks, and anakinra is described as currently EMA-approved for Still's disease at all ages.
