**Scope:** Monitoring and management statements in the stored ESsCD 2025 coeliac guideline part 2. Diagnosis is part 1 and is not in this stored document. Document 7210 is a gastric-ultrasound paper and was not used. The topic spelling Celiac is kept; the stored text says CeD and coeliac.

## Synopsis

The stored part 2 text puts the daily gluten threshold at no more than 10 mg, and it says only certified gluten-free oats should be recommended and can be included from diagnosis. At diagnosis the dietitian educates on the lifelong gluten-free diet and assesses nutrition, and follow-up by a coeliac-specialised dietitian continues that work. Nutritional assessment combines clinical, anthropometric, dietary and laboratory information. Thyroid function is checked with TSH at diagnosis, and pneumococcal vaccination is recommended for stated higher-risk groups. A formal transfer of adolescent care uses a transition letter or a coeliac passport.
