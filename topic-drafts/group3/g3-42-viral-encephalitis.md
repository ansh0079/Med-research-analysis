# Viral encephalitis: evidence record (group 3, not published)

**Status:** draft record, not published. No production load was run.

**Lead document:** Management of suspected viral encephalitis in adults--Association of British Neurologists and British Infection Association National Guidelines. Stored document id 1590. PMID none stored. Database year 2012. Evidence tier stored as guideline.

**Topics this record serves:**
- Viral encephalitis: HSV encephalitis, aciclovir, CSF PCR, MRI findings, empirical approach

**Scope:** Empirical aciclovir, lumbar puncture, CSF PCR, HIV testing and the stored description of early MRI changes in the 2012 ABN/BIA adult guideline. Document 6862 is a steroid meta-analysis and was not used for these recommendations. Hyphenation and the stored grade marks are left as printed.

## Synopsis

The stored guideline says intravenous aciclovir 10 mg/kg three times daily should be started if CSF or imaging suggests viral encephalitis, within 6 hours of admission if those results will not be available, or if the patient is very unwell or deteriorating. All patients with suspected encephalitis should have a lumbar puncture as soon as possible unless it is contraindicated, and PCR for the common viruses. Aciclovir is not stopped because of one negative CSF HSV PCR if encephalitis is still suspected, the dose is reduced in pre-existing renal impairment, and treatment continues for 14 e21 days, as the stored line is printed, with a repeat lumbar puncture. Early MRI changes are described in the cingulate gyrus and medial temporal lobe, including gyral oedema on T1 and high signal on T2 and FLAIR. An HIV test is recommended in all patients with encephalitis or suspected encephalitis.
