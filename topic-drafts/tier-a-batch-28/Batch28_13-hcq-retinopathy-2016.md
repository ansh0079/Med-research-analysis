# Hydroxychloroquine retinopathy: evidence record (batch 28, not published)

**Lead guideline:** Recommendations on Screening for Chloroquine and Hydroxychloroquine Retinopathy (2016 Revision) (2016), PMID 26992838, document id 4497. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The 2016 American Academy of Ophthalmology revision, as stored, recommends a maximum daily hydroxychloroquine dose of 5.0 mg/kg or less of real weight, and 2.3 mg/kg or less of real weight for chloroquine. At recommended doses the text puts the risk of toxicity under 1% up to 5 years and under 2% up to 10 years, rising to almost 20% after 20 years. Major additional risks named are renal disease and tamoxifen. Do a baseline fundus examination to rule out preexisting maculopathy, and begin annual screening after 5 years if the dose is acceptable and there are no major risk factors. Primary tests are automated visual fields plus spectral-domain OCT, looking beyond the central macula in Asian patients. Retinopathy is not reversible and there is no present therapy. Questionable results should be repeated before the drug is stopped. Patients and prescribers should be told the risk, the dose, and the need for annual screening.
