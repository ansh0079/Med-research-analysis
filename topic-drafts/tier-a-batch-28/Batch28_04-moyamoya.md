# Moyamoya disease: clinical presentations, surgical revascularisation indications: evidence record (batch 28, not published)

**Lead guideline:** Guidelines for diagnosis and treatment of moyamoya disease (spontaneous occlusion of the circle of Willis) (2012), PMID 22870528, document id 7186. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The 2012 Japanese guidelines diagnose moyamoya disease from stenosis or occlusion of the terminal intracranial internal carotid artery or the proximal anterior and/or middle cerebral arteries, abnormal vascular networks nearby, and bilateral findings. MRI and MRA can meet the criteria, but conventional angiography should still be done when surgery is planned. Cerebral blood-flow studies are graded for judging the severity of ischemia. Revascularisation is graded as effective for moyamoya disease with cerebral ischemic symptoms. For some other situations, including hemorrhagic disease, the same document says adequate scientific evidence is still lacking.

### Diagnosis

6. the diagnosis of moyamoya disease is made based on the following: (1) stenosis or occlusion of the terminal portion of the intracranial internal carotid artery or the proximal portion of the anterior and/or middle cerebral arteries; (2) abnormal vascular networks in the vicinity of the occlusive or stenotic lesions in the arterial phase; and (3) bilaterality of findings (1) and (2)
