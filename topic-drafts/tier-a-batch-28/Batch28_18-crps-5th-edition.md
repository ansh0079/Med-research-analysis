## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the 5th edition of the practical diagnostic and treatment guidelines (Pain Medicine 2022). The database year 2008 is the source_year field, not the edition. Diagnosis uses the Budapest criteria adopted by the IASP in 2012. The guideline is written as narrative advice rather than numbered grades. The directives below are the sentences in the stored text that tell the clinician what should or should not be done. Sentences about how the authors wrote the paper were left out.
