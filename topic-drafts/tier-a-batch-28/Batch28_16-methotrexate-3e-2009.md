# Methotrexate toxicity and monitoring: evidence record (batch 28, not published)

**Lead guideline:** Multinational evidence-based recommendations for the use of methotrexate in rheumatic disorders with a focus on rheumatoid arthritis: integrating systematic literature research and expert opinion of a broad international panel of rheumatologists in the 3E Initiative (2009), PMID 19033291, document id 1580. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The 2009 3E recommendations cover starting and monitoring methotrexate, focused on rheumatoid arthritis. Work-up includes alcohol intake, education, AST, ALT, albumin, blood count, creatinine, and a chest radiograph within the past year, and considers viral serology, glucose, lipids, and a pregnancy test. Oral methotrexate starts at 10-15 mg/week, rising by 5 mg every 2-4 weeks up to 20-30 mg/week, with a switch to parenteral treatment if response or tolerance is inadequate. At least 5 mg folic acid per week is strongly recommended. Blood tests are every 1-1.5 months until the dose is stable, then every 1-3 months. Stop methotrexate if ALT or AST is confirmed above three times the upper limit of normal. It can be continued through elective orthopaedic surgery in rheumatoid arthritis. It should not be used for at least 3 months before a planned pregnancy, in men or women, and not during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
