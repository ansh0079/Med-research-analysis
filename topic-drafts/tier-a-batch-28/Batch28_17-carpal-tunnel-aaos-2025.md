# Carpal tunnel syndrome: diagnosis and management: evidence record (batch 28, not published)

**Lead guideline:** American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons/ASSH Clinical Practice Guideline Summary Management of Carpal Tunnel Syndrome (2025), PMID 39637428, document id 1419. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The spreadsheet lead is a 2008 narrative review. This record uses the 2025 AAOS/ASSH guideline summary. A strong recommendation means the supporting evidence is high and should be followed unless there is a clear reason not to. Moderate evidence says MRI and upper-limb neurodynamic testing should not be used for diagnosis. The other evidence statements in the summary are listed as printed, including what they say about treatment. The stored text is the summary, so detail that exists only in the full guideline is not here.
