# Tourette syndrome: evidence record (batch 28, not published)

**Lead guideline:** European clinical guidelines for Tourette syndrome and other tic disorders-version 2.0. Part I: assessment (2022), PMID 34661764, document id 1. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The 2011 file on the spreadsheet is only the series introduction and states no treatment recommendations. The stored 2022 text used here is Part I, on assessment. It recommends a physical and especially neurological examination when needed to exclude other neurological disease. Neuroimaging, EEG, and further tests should be done only when the clinical picture requires them, because they do not add value for diagnosing a tic disorder. It recommends Tourette-specific quality-of-life scales. The category of persistent tic disorder is specified as at least one vocal or two motor tics. Drug doses, behavioural therapy protocols, and deep-brain stimulation are not in this part.
