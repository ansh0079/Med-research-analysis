# Flow cytometry in haematology: evidence record (batch 28, not published)

**Lead guideline:** Guidelines on the use of multicolour flow cytometry in the diagnosis of haematological neoplasms. British Committee for Standards in Haematology (2014), PMID 24620735, document id 7306. Read in full from the stored text.

## Synopsis (gist)

The BCSH guideline's own summary of key recommendations is the list below, with the evidence grade in parentheses. It covers cytometer set-up (lasers, PMT voltages, compensation, daily QC), panel design and cocktail validation, sample age and quality, acquisition, analysis, and reporting for haematological neoplasms. Three very short numbered points failed the 40-character quote check and are not listed. The recommendations are technical standards for the laboratory, not treatment guidelines for a named leukaemia.
