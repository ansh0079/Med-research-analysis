## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the German S2k guideline on diagnosis and treatment of Hymenoptera venom allergy (PMID 37854067). Recommendations are consensus statements with a strength-of-consensus number. They cover sting provocation, large local reactions, venom immunotherapy indications, maintenance treatment, and reduction of side effects, in the wording of each numbered statement. Database source_year is empty; the PMID is 2023 and 2023 is used here. No other venom-allergy guideline was in the database.
