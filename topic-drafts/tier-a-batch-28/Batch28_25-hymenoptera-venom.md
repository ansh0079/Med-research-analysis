## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the German S2k guideline on diagnosis and treatment of Hymenoptera venom allergy (PMID 37854067). Recommendations are consensus statements with a strength-of-consensus number. They cover sting provocation, large local reactions, venom immunotherapy indications, maintenance treatment, and reduction of side effects, in the wording of each numbered statement. Database source_year is empty; the PMID is 2023 and 2023 is used here. No other venom-allergy guideline was in the database.

### S2k recommendations

   *Anchor:* "Risk factors for an increased sting risk shall be obtained when taking the medical history."

5. If an unequivocal diagnosis is obtained by in-vitro diagnostics, a skin test can be omitted.  
   *Anchor:* "If an unequivocal diagnosis is obtained by in-vitro diagnostics, a skin test can be omitted."
6. Diagnostic sting provocations (before the start of VIT) or sting provocations after completion of VIT shall not be performed.  
   *Anchor:* "Diagnostic sting provocations (before the start of VIT) or sting provocations after completion of VIT shall not be performed."
7. Acute treatment can be symptomatic using non-sedating antihistamines, cooling compresses, topical and/or systemic glucocorticoids.  
   *Anchor:* "Acute treatment can be symptomatic using non-sedating antihistamines, cooling compresses, topical and/or systemic glucocorticoids."
8. VIT shall not be performed for large local reactions.  
   *Anchor:* "VIT shall not be performed for large local reactions."

11. VIT shall be performed in patients with a history of an anaphylactic reaction of severity grade ≥ II according to Ring and Messmer, and with evidence of IgE-mediated sensitization to the culprit venom.  
   *Anchor:* "VIT shall be performed in patients with a history of an anaphylactic reaction of severity grade ≥ II according to Ring and Messmer, and with evidence of IgE-mediated sensitization to the culprit venom."
12. Sting provocation can be performed on a case-by-case basis to verify the success of therapy. Provocation shall only be performed in patients who have reached the planned maintenance dose and tolerate VIT.

13. If treatment failure is evident during ACE inhibitor therapy, discontinuation of the ACE inhibitor should be considered.  
   *Anchor:* "If treatment failure is evident during ACE inhibitor therapy, discontinuation of the ACE inhibitor should be considered."
14. If HVA requiring absolutely necessary treatment is suspected, and if results from IgE detection methods for venom components and whole venom and from skin tests are not conclusive, cellular tests can be performed.  
   *Anchor:* "If HVA requiring absolutely necessary treatment is suspected, and if results from IgE detection methods for venom components and whole venom and from skin tests are not conclusive, cellular tests can be performed."
15. The use of β-blockers and ACE inhibitors are no contraindication of VIT. Patients should be informed about possible interactions.
