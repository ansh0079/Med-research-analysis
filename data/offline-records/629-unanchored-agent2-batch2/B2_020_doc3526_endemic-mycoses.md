## Synopsis (gist)

This open-label randomized trial compared super-bioavailability itraconazole with conventional itraconazole for endemic fungal infections caused by Histoplasma, Blastomyces, Coccidioides, or Sporothrix. The source states that current guidelines recommend itraconazole as the primary or an alternative first-line agent for non-life-threatening disease caused by these endemic fungi, or as step-down therapy after amphotericin B for life-threatening infection. Clinical success was similar for the two formulations, and super-bioavailability itraconazole was reported as bioequivalent, well tolerated, and efficacious, with a more favorable safety profile. Patients receiving super-bioavailability itraconazole had less variability in blood levels and fewer adverse events than patients receiving conventional itraconazole. The authors suggest that super-bioavailability itraconazole achieves clinical outcomes similar to conventional itraconazole. The observed outcomes suggest that it is a welcome addition to the antifungal armamentarium, and the trial excluded meningitis, paracoccidioidomycosis, and talaromycosis.
