## Synopsis (gist)

In the 2023 ESC acute coronary syndrome guideline, a primary PCI strategy is recommended after resuscitated cardiac arrest when the ECG shows persistent ST-segment elevation or equivalents (Class I, Level B). Routine immediate angiography is not recommended in haemodynamically stable patients without persistent ST-segment elevation (Class III, Level A). Delayed rather than immediate angiography should be considered in haemodynamically stable patients without ST-segment elevation after out-of-hospital cardiac arrest (Class IIa, Level B). Health systems should transfer patients in whom acute coronary syndrome is suspected after resuscitated arrest directly to a hospital that offers primary PCI around the clock (Class I, Level C). Transport to a cardiac arrest centre according to local protocols should be considered (Class IIa, Level C).
