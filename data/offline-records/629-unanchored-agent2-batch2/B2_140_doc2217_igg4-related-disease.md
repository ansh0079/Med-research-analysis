## Synopsis (gist)

This UEG and SGF guideline gives GRADE recommendations on IgG4-related digestive disease and also describes IgG4-related disease as a systemic condition. IgG4 serum level alone lacks sensitivity and specificity but should be measured if IgG4-related gastrointestinal disease is suspected (GRADE 2C, strong agreement). Glucocorticoid treatment should be weight-based, started orally at 0.6–0.8 mg/kg body weight per day for 1 month to induce remission, then tapered within two additional months, with response assessed at week 2–4. Maintenance glucocorticoids should be considered in multi-organ disease or after relapse, the diagnosis should be reconsidered if activity and burden are unchanged within 3 months, and immunosuppressive drugs should be added if the disease relapses during those 3 months. Patients with systemic manifestations should be followed with serological tests, imaging, and the IgG4-RD Responder Index, and life-long follow-up is advisable (GRADE 1B, strong agreement).
