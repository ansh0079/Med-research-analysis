## Synopsis (gist)

The Enduring Consensus Cervical Cancer Screening and Management Guidelines Committee issued recommendations for HPV testing of self-collected vaginal specimens in health care settings. Clinician-collected cervical specimens are preferred, and self-collected vaginal specimens are acceptable for cervical cancer screening, with repeat testing in 3 years recommended after an HPV-negative self-collected screen. When a self-collected specimen is positive for HPV 16 and/or 18, direct referral for colposcopy with concurrent cytology collection is recommended. For untyped HPV, HPV HR12 other than 16/18, or HPV 45, 33/58, 31, 52, 35/39/68, or 51, a clinician-collected specimen for cytology or dual stain is recommended, and HPV types 56/59/66 without other carcinogenic types are managed with repeat testing in 1 year and colposcopy if still positive. In the surveillance setting, clinician-collected cervical specimens are preferred, and a self-collected specimen is acceptable only when a clinician-collected specimen cannot be obtained, with management then following the 2019 guidelines. Assays and collection devices that were not FDA-approved for self-collected vaginal specimens should not be used for clinical care.
