## Synopsis (gist)

The Nordic Anal Cancer group consensus guideline addresses risk-adapted delineation of the elective clinical target volume in anal cancer, rather than the full diagnosis and treatment of the disease. The main curative treatment is chemoradiotherapy, and the aim of this work was to establish guidelines for delineation of the elective clinical target volume. The authors present consensus-based recommendations on which regions to include and how those regions should be delineated, and some recommendations deviate from current international guidelines. For the external iliac region and the cranial border, two different recommendations are specified and both are considered acceptable, including a novel risk-adapted alternative. For patients with small (< 1 cm) T1N0 perianal tumors not involving the anal canal, the authors believe elective nodal irradiation probably can be omitted, but firm recommendations await data from the ongoing ACT3 trial. The posterolateral part of the inguinal region is excluded, decreasing the volume of irradiated normal tissue.
