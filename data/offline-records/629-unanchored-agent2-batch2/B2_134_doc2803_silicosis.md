## Synopsis (gist)

This AWMF S2k guideline is a translated framework for diagnosing and assessing occupational quartz-dust lung disease, including silicosis as occupational disease 4101, its causal link to exposure, and functional consequences such as reduction of earning capacity. It states that PA chest radiography has significant limitations for the diagnosis of silicosis and that a qualified low-dose HRCT is recommended and should be reported in a standardized manner using the ICOERD classification. Peripheral oxygen saturation should not be used as the sole method for analyzing gas exchange abnormalities, and repeat imaging in follow-up expert assessments is required only if medically justified. If lung carcinoma develops in an insured person with silicosis, suspicion of the related occupational lung-cancer disease should be raised and reported, and recognized cases should undergo regular follow-up for medical rehabilitation. Individual preventive measures to avoid further inhalational exposure should be considered, and pharmacological therapies such as rituximab or plasmapheresis are described as reserved for refractory cases.
