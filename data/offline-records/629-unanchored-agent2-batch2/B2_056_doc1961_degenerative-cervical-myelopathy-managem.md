## Synopsis (gist)

This review of recent degenerative cervical myelopathy literature grades new recommendations as strong or conditional. It cites the 2017 AO Spine guidance for a strong recommendation that surgery be undertaken for severe and moderate disease, and describes uncertainty between nonoperative observation and surgery for mild disease. A conditional recommendation supports using diffusion-weighted imaging MR signal changes to evaluate prognosis after surgery, and a conditional recommendation is that understanding MRI findings helps counsel patients about expected recovery. A conditional recommendation is that surgery should be offered as an option for mild disease, while structured follow-up remains a viable option in the clinically stable patient because comparative evidence does not support a strong recommendation in favour of surgery. Where either an anterior or a posterior approach could be used, a strong recommendation states equipoise in outcomes, the differing adverse event profile should be considered in shared decision-making, and a conditional recommendation is that clinicians consider pain, medical frailty, and health-related quality of life.
