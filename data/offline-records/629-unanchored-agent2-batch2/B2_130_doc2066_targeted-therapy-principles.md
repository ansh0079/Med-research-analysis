## Synopsis (gist)

These Chinese guidelines address HER2-targeted therapy in gastric and gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma. IHC should be employed as the standard method for HER2 protein expression and reported as IHC 0, 1+, 2+, or 3+, and ISH is the definitive method for gene amplification, with FISH mandatory when IHC is 2+. For advanced gastric cancer with HER2 IHC 3+ or IHC 2+/FISH+ and PD-L1 CPS < 1, trastuzumab plus chemotherapy is recommended as first-line therapy (strong), and when PD-L1 CPS is ≥ 1, addition of pembrolizumab to trastuzumab and chemotherapy is recommended (strong). Trastuzumab is not routinely recommended for continuation beyond progression, second-line trastuzumab with paclitaxel is recommended for patients who have not previously received trastuzumab, and T-DXd is recommended as second-line therapy for patients who have previously received treatment. Disitamab vedotin is recommended as third-line or later-line therapy for HER2 IHC 3+ or IHC 2+/FISH+ and for IHC 2+/FISH−, and T-DXd is also recommended in the third or later line. For resistance to HER2-targeted therapy, participation in clinical trials is recommended, and in locally advanced disease participation in perioperative HER2-targeted therapy trials is encouraged.
