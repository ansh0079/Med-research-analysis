## Synopsis (gist)

This 2023 guideline on septic arthritis in native joints says synovial fluid should be aspirated as soon as septic arthritis is suspected, and analysed first for bacteria, then the white-cell count with the polymorphonuclear percentage, then crystals. A count above 50 000 cells per microlitre suggests septic arthritis but does not confirm it, and a count below 25 000 cells per microlitre lowers the probability without excluding it. Those cut-offs may not apply in immunosuppressed patients, and crystals do not rule out infection. Culture of synovial fluid is recommended, with the remaining fluid inoculated into blood-culture bottles, a Gram stain performed, plating on agar and enrichment broth, and incubation for 5 to 7 days. Viscosity is not discussed.
