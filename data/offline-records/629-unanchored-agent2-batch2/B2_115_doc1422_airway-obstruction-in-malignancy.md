## Synopsis (gist)

These World Association for Bronchology and Interventional Pulmonology guidelines address airway stenting for malignant central airway obstruction. In patients with symptomatic malignant central airway obstruction, the panel suggests that airway stenting be performed to improve quality of life or performance status (Grade 2B). Airway stenting can lead to improvement in survival and therefore the panel suggests that it be pursued in the presence of attributable symptoms (Grade 2C). In patients with respiratory failure requiring invasive mechanical ventilation due to malignant central airway obstruction, the panel recommends that airway stenting be considered, as it may lead to early liberation from ventilatory support (Grade 1C). In patients who require airway stenting, the panel suggests pulmonary hygiene measures to reduce stent-related complications and suggests surveillance bronchoscopies to detect complications in asymptomatic patients (Grade 2C). The panel suggests that either silicone or hybrid metallic stents be used (Grade 2B).
