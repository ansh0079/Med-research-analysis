## Synopsis (gist)

This review treats hydroxyapatite deposition disease as crystal accumulation in soft tissue with inflammation, and it discusses imaging and treatment. Imaging specialists may perform image-guided needle aspiration or lavage to remove deposits, inserting a needle under imaging guidance and aspirating the calcific material. The conclusion says treatment combines conservative and interventionist approaches and is tailored to disease severity and the patient's overall health. Radiologists are described as central to diagnosis and treatment, within a multidisciplinary approach. The same text says the treatment used for calcium pyrophosphate deposition, including NSAIDs, corticosteroids, or colchicine, varies markedly from the approach for hydroxyapatite deposition disease.
