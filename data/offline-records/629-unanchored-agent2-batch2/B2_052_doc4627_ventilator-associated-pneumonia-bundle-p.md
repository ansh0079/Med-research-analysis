## Synopsis (gist)

Hospital-acquired pneumonia and ventilator-associated pneumonia are defined by onset after at least 48 hours, and ventilator-associated pneumonia also requires tracheal intubation and mechanical ventilation for at least 48 hours. The review states that clinical diagnosis alone may miss about one-third of ventilator-associated pneumonia and that guidelines recommend clinical findings plus lower-respiratory-tract cultures. A bundle of care is recommended for prevention, including non-invasive ventilation when suitable, early enteral nutrition, and circuit changes only in selected cases. Constant circuit changes are not generally recommended. Empirical intravenous antibiotics are the first treatment step and are guided by risk of multidrug-resistant pathogens and mortality risk, with narrow-spectrum monotherapy when that risk is low and combination therapy, including an anti-MRSA agent, in defined high-risk situations. Definitive therapy should follow culture results, and the review states that antibacterial treatment should not exceed 7 days in the majority of cases.
