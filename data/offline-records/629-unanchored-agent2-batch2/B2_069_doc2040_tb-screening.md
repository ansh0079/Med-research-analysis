## Synopsis (gist)

Biologic medications for immune-mediated inflammatory diseases may increase the risk of tuberculosis reactivation, but data on screening for TB in low TB prevalence areas are limited. In this single-center retrospective study, 4,085 adult patients prescribed a biologic medication were included, 3024 (74.0%) had at least one screening TB test, and 42 were positive. Among patients treated with tumor necrosis factor-alpha inhibitors, 1779 of 2129 patients (83.6%) underwent TB testing and 25 (1.4%) were positive. Nine out of 13 society guidelines recommend TB screening for TNFα inhibitors but have differing recommendations for other biologic medications. The authors recommend initial TB screening for patients starting treatment with TNFα inhibitors and suggest initial screening for patients starting JAK inhibitors. After an initial negative TB assessment, annual retesting of patients with low risk of TB exposure should be avoided.
