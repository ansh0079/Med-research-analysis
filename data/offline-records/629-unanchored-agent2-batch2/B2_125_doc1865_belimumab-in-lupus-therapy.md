## Synopsis (gist)

The 2023 EULAR update gives recommendations for systemic lupus erythematosus and includes belimumab among the biologics used for extrarenal disease and lupus nephritis. In patients not responding to hydroxychloroquine, or unable to reduce glucocorticoids below doses acceptable for chronic use, addition of immunosuppressive agents and/or biologic agents, for example belimumab or anifrolumab, should be considered. The recommendation states there is no hierarchy in the choice between anifrolumab and belimumab, and that prior use of a conventional immunosuppressive drug should not be mandatory for initiating anifrolumab or belimumab. For active proliferative lupus nephritis, glucocorticoids with mycophenolate or low-dose intravenous cyclophosphamide are recommended as anchor drugs, and combination therapy with belimumab should be considered. Belimumab should always be given in combination with mycophenolate or low-dose cyclophosphamide as initial therapy and with mycophenolate or azathioprine as maintenance therapy. For severe neuropsychiatric disease, anifrolumab and belimumab are not recommended, and for active skin disease belimumab is listed as second-line therapy.
