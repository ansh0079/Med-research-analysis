## Synopsis (gist)

This sub-analysis of PARADIGM-HF compared sacubitril/valsartan with enalapril in Indian patients with chronic heart failure and reduced ejection fraction and compared treatment effects with the global cohort. The primary outcome occurred in 21.81% of the sacubitril/valsartan group and 24.76% of the enalapril group (HR 0.89; 95% confidence interval 0.646–1.231). Cardiovascular death occurred in 17.45% and 20.63%, and first hospitalization for heart failure in 7.48% and 9.52%, with all-cause mortality 19.0% and 21.9%. The authors conclude that sacubitril/valsartan effectively reduced the risks of cardiovascular death, heart failure hospitalization, and all-cause mortality compared with enalapril, with an acceptable safety profile, and that benefits were similar in the Indian and global cohorts. The key message states that sacubitril/valsartan was superior to enalapril and safe for those outcomes and in concordance with the global trial. The sub-analysis is presented as evidence for acceptance of sacubitril/valsartan as a treatment option for the Indian population.
