## Synopsis (gist)

This is a focused GRADE update of the Society of Critical Care Medicine guidelines for pain, anxiety, agitation/sedation, delirium, immobility, and sleep disruption in adult ICU patients. The task force issued conditional recommendations to use dexmedetomidine over propofol for sedation, to provide enhanced mobilization/rehabilitation, and to administer melatonin. We suggest using dexmedetomidine over propofol for sedation in mechanically ventilated adults where light sedation and/or a reduction in delirium are of highest priorities (conditional recommendation; moderate certainty of evidence). We are unable to issue a recommendation for or against antipsychotics over usual care for the treatment of delirium in adult ICU patients (conditional recommendation; low certainty of evidence). We suggest administering melatonin over no melatonin in adult patients admitted to the ICU (conditional recommendation; low certainty), based on reduction of delirium prevalence and perceived improvement in sleep quality balanced against the low risk of adverse events.

## Hand-back notes

CAM-ICU and other delirium screening instruments are not addressed. This focused update answers selected questions on benzodiazepines for anxiety, dexmedetomidine versus propofol, antipsychotics for established delirium, enhanced mobilization, and melatonin. A complete delirium prevention bundle and nonpharmacologic prevention protocols other than those questions are out of scope. Recommendations below are the update statements that direct delirium-related sedation, antipsychotic treatment, and melatonin use.
