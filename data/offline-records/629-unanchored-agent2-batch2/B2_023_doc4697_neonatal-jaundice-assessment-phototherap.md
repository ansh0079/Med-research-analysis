## Synopsis (gist)

This prospective study managed term and late-preterm infants with the 2022 American Academy of Pediatrics hyperbilirubinemia guideline and recorded phototherapy, follow-up, readmission, exchange transfusion, and acute bilirubin encephalopathy. Implementation of the 2022 guideline reduces phototherapy use and unnecessary follow-up visits without increasing adverse outcomes. No cases of acute bilirubin encephalopathy or exchange transfusion were reported. Together, the findings suggest that the 2022 guideline safely reduces phototherapy and unnecessary follow-up while maintaining protection against severe bilirubin-related complications within a structured follow-up system. The authors state that the 2022 guideline appears to safely reduce phototherapy use without increasing severe complications when coupled with a structured, risk-based follow-up system. By minimizing unnecessary phototherapy, clinicians may reduce maternal-infant separation and its associated adverse effects, including impaired bonding, reduced breastfeeding success, maternal depression, and potential long term neurologic complications.
