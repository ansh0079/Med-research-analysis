## Synopsis (gist)

The Saudi guideline, developed with the GRADE ADOLOPMENT approach from NICE guideline NG59, addresses assessment and management of low back pain and sciatica in adults and does not cover serious spinal pathology, inflammatory back pain, progressive neurological deficit, or suspected cauda equina syndrome. In patients with low back pain and/or sciatica, the Task Force recommends validated risk assessment tools such as the STarT Back Screening Tool after clinical examination, a strong recommendation with very low certainty of evidence. Those tools should be considered at the first clinical encounter to help identify patients who may need further treatment. Imaging should be guided by clinical assessment rather than used as a screening tool. For non-specific low back pain and/or sciatica, the Task Force suggests return-to-work programmes, a conditional recommendation with low certainty of evidence, and the findings state that management should be guided by clinical assessment, with restricted use of imaging and appropriate use of psychological, multidisciplinary, and procedural interventions.
