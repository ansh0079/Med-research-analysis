## Synopsis (gist)

The document used for this topic is the 2019 review of anemia of inflammation, which the text also calls anemia of chronic disease. It describes this as the most frequent anemia in hospitalized and chronically ill patients, and as the second most prevalent anemia worldwide after iron deficiency. Hepcidin, induced in inflammation, is described as blocking intestinal iron absorption and holding iron in reticuloendothelial cells, which restricts erythropoiesis. The review states that iron therapy combined with erythropoiesis-stimulating agents can improve the anemia in many patients. The paper is a narrative review and does not set graded practice recommendations.
