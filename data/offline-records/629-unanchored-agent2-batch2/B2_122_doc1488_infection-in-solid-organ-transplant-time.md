## Synopsis (gist)

These Korean Society of Infectious Diseases and Korean Society for Transplantation guidelines address prevention of cytomegalovirus infection after solid organ transplantation and use GRADE to label recommendations as strong or weak. All organ donors and recipients should be screened for CMV IgG serostatus before or at the time of transplantation, and the combined interpretation of donor and recipient serology should be used to categorize posttransplant CMV risk and guide prevention. For CMV D+/R-, antiviral prophylaxis is recommended (strong, high); for CMV D-/R-, CMV prophylaxis is not recommended; and for CMV R+ kidney, liver, and heart recipients, preemptive therapy is recommended, although antiviral prophylaxis may be considered, while antiviral prophylaxis is recommended for lung recipients. Valganciclovir is the preferred drug for antiviral prophylaxis for both adults and children, intravenous ganciclovir is an alternative when vascular access is available, and low-dose valganciclovir is not recommended. Quantitative nucleic acid testing is recommended to guide preemptive therapy, thresholds should be defined independently in each center, and CMV monitoring at least once weekly for 3 months after transplantation is recommended for preemptive therapy. In children under 12 months, CMV R+ should be interpreted as CMV R- because of possible transplacental maternal antibodies, and valganciclovir dosage should be calculated from body surface area.
