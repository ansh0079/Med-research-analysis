## Synopsis (gist)

This ASPEN guideline gives nutrition recommendations for adults with head and neck cancer, in whom maintaining nutrition is important because malnutrition and muscle wasting contribute to worse outcomes. The panel recommends that all patients be screened for malnutrition with a validated tool and that patients at risk undergo a comprehensive nutrition assessment by a dietitian or other qualified nutrition professional. For patients receiving any treatment modality, the guideline recommends a protein intake of 1.2–1.5 g/kg/day and an energy intake of at least 30 kcal/kg/day, estimated from actual body weight, with ongoing monitoring of intake and nutrition status. Enteral feeding is suggested when nutrition intake or status is compromised despite oral strategies, postoperative nutrition intake is suggested within 24 h, and the choice of a PEG or RIG tube versus an NGT should be made through interdisciplinary discussion. Weekly consultation with a dietitian during radiotherapy, and every 2 weeks for up to 6 weeks after treatment, is recommended, and consultation by a speech pathologist is recommended when treatment is likely to affect swallowing. Oral or enteral glutamine may be acceptable at the discretion of the interdisciplinary team, and the guideline suggests not adding parenteral glutamine to standard nutrition therapy until further research confirms its safety.

20. Oral/enteral glutamine has been shown to reduce the severity of oral mucositis, with the potential to reduce other treatment toxicities and hospitalization, and improve treatment completion. We therefore suggest that the use of oral/enteral glutamine in patients with head and neck cancer may be acceptable at the discretion of the interdisciplinary team. Intravenous glutamine is more controversial because of one small study that reported increased mortality in the patients receiving intravenous glutamine and recent preclinical trials suggesting mechanisms through which glutamine may contribute to tumor growth and treatment resistance.
