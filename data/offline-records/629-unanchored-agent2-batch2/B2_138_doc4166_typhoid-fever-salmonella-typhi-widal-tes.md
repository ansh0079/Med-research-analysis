## Synopsis (gist)

This Cochrane review assesses vaccines for preventing typhoid fever caused by Salmonella Typhi. Two vaccines are described as widely available, oral Ty21a and parenteral Vi polysaccharide, and newer typhoid conjugate vaccines are at varying stages of development and use. The World Health Organization has recently recommended a Vi tetanus toxoid conjugate vaccine, Typbar-TCV, as the preferred vaccine for all ages. A three-dose schedule of Ty21a probably prevents around half of typhoid cases during the first three years after vaccination, which the review rates as moderate-certainty evidence, and fever is probably more common after vaccination than with placebo. A single dose of Vi polysaccharide vaccine prevents around two-thirds of typhoid cases in the first year (high-certainty evidence), while second-year results were more variable and the vaccine probably prevented between 45% and 69% of cases (moderate-certainty evidence).
