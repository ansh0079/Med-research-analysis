## Synopsis (gist)

This network meta-analysis compared prevention methods for contrast-associated acute kidney injury in 200 trials, 42,273 patients, and 44 interventions. The primary outcome was a creatinine rise of at least 25% or 0.5 mg/dl from baseline, measured one to five days after contrast. Intravenous sodium chloride hydration was the reference treatment. In the main analysis the highest ranks were allopurinol (SUCRA 0.9647), prostaglandin E1 (0.7809), and oxygen (0.7527); intravenous hydration ranked lower than those three and above placebo (0.3124 versus 0.2694). The authors say those higher-ranked options deserve further evaluation in larger trials.
