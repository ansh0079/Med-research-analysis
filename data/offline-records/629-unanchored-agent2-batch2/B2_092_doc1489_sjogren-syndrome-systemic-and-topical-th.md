## Synopsis (gist)

The stored text is the summary of the 2019 EULAR recommendations for Sjögren syndrome treated with topical and systemic medicines. The task force endorsed three overarching consensus-based recommendations and 12 specific recommendations, ordered from dryness, fatigue, and pain through to systemic disease. The summary names topical oral saliva substitutes and ocular treatments: artificial tears, topical non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, topical corticosteroids, topical CyA, and serum tear drops. It also names oral muscarinic agonists (pilocarpine and cevimeline), hydroxychloroquine, oral glucocorticoids, synthetic immunosuppressants, and the biologics rituximab, abatacept, and belimumab. Levels of evidence are described as mostly modest, and task-force agreement as mostly very high. The individual wording of the 12 graded recommendations is not in this stored copy.
