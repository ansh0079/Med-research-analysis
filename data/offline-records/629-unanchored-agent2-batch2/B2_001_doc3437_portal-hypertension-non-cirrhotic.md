## Synopsis (gist)

Studies of outcomes in pregnant patients with non-cirrhotic portal hypertension are limited, and this systematic review and meta-analysis included twelve studies published from 1999 through December 2021. The pooled rates of variceal bleeding, ascites, and severe anemia requiring blood transfusion were 9.6%, 2.3%, and 14.9%. The pooled rates of spontaneous miscarriage, gestational hypertension, delivery by cesarean section, and postpartum hemorrhage were 11.9%, 4.5%, 36.7%, and 4.7%. The pooled stillbirth rate was 2.5%, and among live births the pooled rates of preterm birth, low birth weight, intensive care unit admission, and neonatal mortality were 21.6%, 18.7%, 15.5%, and 1.8%. Pregnancy in these patients is associated with increased maternal and fetal morbidity, but mortality remains low.
