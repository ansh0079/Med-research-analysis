## Synopsis (gist)

This systematic review examines nursing management of chemotherapy extravasation, risk factors, and training programmes. The authors report that management is closely tied to nursing factors and that nurses have a role in treatment, prevention, and health education. Deficient nursing knowledge is described as a contributor to extravasation, and vascular-trauma indicators include decreased elasticity, pain, and signs of infection. Training is described as improving competence and patient safety, and one included programme was followed by a fall in patient complaints related to extravasation from 20% to 8%. The conclusions state that up-to-date knowledge and adequate training can identify risk factors, prevent more extravasations, and improve health results. One included pediatric study reported saline wash as a safe and easy process that reduced the rate of skin necrosis.
