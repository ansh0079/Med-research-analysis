## Synopsis (gist)

This crossover trial tested hybrid closed-loop insulin delivery in people with type 2 diabetes already treated with continuous subcutaneous insulin infusion and continuous glucose monitoring. In those patients, implementation of a hybrid closed-loop system improved time in range, with a reduction in time above range, glucose variability, and the glucose management indicator, and without an increase in time below range. Quality of sleep was better during closed-loop therapy because sleep fragmentation was reduced. The authors summarize that hybrid closed-loop therapy further improves glycemic control in people with type 2 diabetes already optimally treated with pump therapy plus continuous glucose monitoring. The source states that use of continuous glucose monitoring metrics, in addition to HbA1c, is now recommended to monitor patients with type 1 diabetes and type 2 diabetes because those metrics capture glucose variability and hypoglycemia risk. The trial is presented as a proof of concept for which insulin delivery modality could provide the best glycemic control in patients with type 2 diabetes.
