## Synopsis (gist)

This Colombian Association of Digestive Endoscopy GRADE guideline compares single-operator cholangioscopy with ERCP for indeterminate biliary strictures and for difficult biliary stones. For adult patients with indeterminate biliary strictures, the panel made a conditional recommendation for single-operator cholangioscopy with stricture pattern characterization over ERCP with brushing and/or biopsy for sensitivity, specificity, and procedure success. Recommendation 1 suggests single-operator cholangioscopy with characterization of the stricture pattern and biopsy over ERCP for primary biliary strictures of undetermined etiology, conditional on moderate certainty for diagnostic accuracy and low certainty for clinical outcomes. For adult patients with difficult biliary stones, the panel suggests single-operator cholangioscopy over ERCP with large-balloon papilla dilation, a conditional recommendation based on low certainty in the evidence of effects. Conditional recommendations are those for which the guideline panel suggests a course, using the GRADE wording in the document.

## Hand-back notes

The source covers single-operator cholangioscopy versus ERCP for indeterminate biliary strictures and, separately, for difficult biliary stones. Stent selection, surgical reconstruction, and malignancy treatment pathways for biliary strictures are outside this comparison. The difficult-stone recommendation is included because the source states it, and it is not stricture management.
