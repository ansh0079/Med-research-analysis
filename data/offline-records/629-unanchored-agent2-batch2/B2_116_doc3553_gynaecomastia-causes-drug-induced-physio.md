## Synopsis (gist)

Gynecomastia is defined as the benign proliferation of male breast glandular tissue. Observation is recommended for physiological or recent-onset cases. Pharmacological therapy may be considered in selected idiopathic or painful forms, whereas testosterone replacement should be restricted to men with proven hypogonadism. Surgery is indicated for long-standing fibrotic gynecomastia or significant psychological distress. The panel recommends carefully reviewing the medication history to identify drugs potentially associated with gynecomastia, and recommends ruling out pathological causes based on clinical history, physical examination, and a tailored diagnostic work-up. We suggest avoiding an extensive diagnostic work-up before ruling out physiological causes of gynecomastia.
