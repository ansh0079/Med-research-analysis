## Synopsis (gist)

A variety of therapeutic strategies are described for vestibular schwannoma, with wait-and-scan and periodic imaging often the initial approach and additional options including bevacizumab, everolimus, lapatinib, radiotherapy, or surgery. Promising bevacizumab results are based solely on neurofibromatosis type 2, and the source states that there remains no safe and effective pharmacological therapy capable of reliably improving tumor size or preserving hearing function. Surgery is described as a good option for tumor control but technically challenging at the cranial base, and radiotherapy carries risks such as radiation-induced tumor formation. The primary molecular driver is biallelic NF2 inactivation, and the molecular pathogenesis is described as incompletely understood. The meta-analysis concludes that it defines a multifaceted transcriptional landscape in vestibular schwannoma and that future single-cell, spatial, longitudinal, and functional studies are essential to inform biomarker development and therapeutic strategies.
