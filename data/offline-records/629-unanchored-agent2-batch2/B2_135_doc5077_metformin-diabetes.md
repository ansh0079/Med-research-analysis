## Synopsis (gist)

This systematic review and meta-analysis examines metformin alone or combined with lifestyle interventions for preventing type 2 diabetes in high-risk metformin-naive patients with prediabetes. The authors conclude that metformin alone or combined with lifestyle interventions may prevent T2DM in patients with prediabetes, and that the combined intervention is more effective in those patients. They also state that metformin may prevent diabetes in patients with obesity and in normal-weight patients. Metformin alone is reported as more effective in Caucasian women and in patients older than 60 years, and metformin with lifestyle interventions as more effective in Asian Indian men and in those younger than 60 years. The combined intervention reduces metformin dosage and may act faster than metformin alone, and a regimen that adds metformin to lifestyle modification should be considered in real life but needs ongoing healthcare support.
