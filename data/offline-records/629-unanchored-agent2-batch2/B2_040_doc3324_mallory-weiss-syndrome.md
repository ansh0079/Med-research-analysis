## Synopsis (gist)

Mallory-Weiss syndrome is described as a gastroesophageal complication with mucosal tearing and bleeding from the mouth or stool. This systematic review and meta-analysis found an overall prevalence of 15.46%, and the conclusion states that prevalence is not extensive among people with gastrointestinal-tract complications. Subjects in the included studies were mostly middle-aged men. Hiatus hernia was strongly associated with the syndrome and was called the most significant risk factor. Alcohol was not a strong predictor, and the analysis describes a weak association. Hiccups were reported as an emerging risk factor associated with the syndrome.
