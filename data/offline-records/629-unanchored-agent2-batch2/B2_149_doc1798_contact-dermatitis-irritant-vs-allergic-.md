## Synopsis (gist)

Longer application periods for patch tests are not recommended. It is recommended to perform at least two readings of the patch test reactions. When a positive reaction has unknown relevance, it is recommended to re-evaluate the history and exposures, repeat the examination, and consider additional tests such as ROATs, use tests, spot tests, chemical analyses, and worksite visits where indicated. It is not recommended to treat a morphologically positive patch test of unknown relevance as a false-positive result. An isolated doubtful reaction suspected to be allergic should be re-tested and/or examined with serial dilutions, because the text states that a doubtful reaction is not always clearly allergic or irritant. Patch testing is recommended in atopic dermatitis when allergic contact dermatitis is suspected, with the caution that atopic skin may produce irritant false-positive reactions.
