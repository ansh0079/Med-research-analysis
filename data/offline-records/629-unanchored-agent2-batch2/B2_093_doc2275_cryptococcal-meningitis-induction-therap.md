## Synopsis (gist)

This extract is from a global guideline on diagnosis and management of cryptococcosis and states induction options for cryptococcal meningitis. Liposomal amphotericin B 3-4 mg/kg daily and flucytosine 25 mg/kg four times a day is the most optimal induction therapy option for cryptococcal meningitis in high-income settings and is strongly recommended in SOT and non-HIV non-SOT settings. In low-income settings, patients with HIV-associated cryptococcal meningitis are best treated with liposomal amphotericin B 10 mg/kg as a single dose, with 14 days of flucytosine 25 mg/kg four times a day and fluconazole 1200 mg daily as induction therapy. The WHO guidelines now recommend the AMBITION-cm regimen as the preferred antifungal therapy in people living with HIV and cryptococcal meningitis. Consider prolonging induction if CSF is persistently culture positive at 2 weeks, and consider extending induction to 4-6 weeks for Cryptococcus gattii CNS infection in non-HIV patients or CNS cryptococcoma. The routine use of high-dose dexamethasone in cryptococcal meningitis is not recommended, and immediate or very early commencement of antiretroviral therapy is not recommended.
