## Synopsis (gist)

This meta-analysis compares tenecteplase with alteplase for intravenous thrombolysis in acute ischemic stroke. Pooled results associated tenecteplase with a greater likelihood of excellent functional recovery at 90 days, a shorter door-to-needle time, and lower mortality, whereas analyses limited to randomized trials showed no significant mortality difference. The abstract concludes that tenecteplase provides efficacy and safety profiles comparable to alteplase, with a greater likelihood of excellent functional recovery, faster treatment initiation, and lower mortality in observational studies. The closing conclusion states that tenecteplase is non-inferior to alteplase and that safety profiles were generally comparable. The source states that the optimal tenecteplase dose is 0.25 mg/kg and that 0.40 mg/kg should be avoided because of symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage. The EXTEND-IA TNK trial is cited as finding higher early reperfusion with tenecteplase than with alteplase before thrombectomy.
