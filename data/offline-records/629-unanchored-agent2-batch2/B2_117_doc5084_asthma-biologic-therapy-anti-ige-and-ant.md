## Synopsis (gist)

This review of biologics for severe asthma lists seven approved agents, including the anti-IgE drug omalizumab and the anti-IL-5 or anti-IL-5-receptor drugs mepolizumab, reslizumab, depemokimab, and benralizumab. Omalizumab is described as the first approved anti-IgE biologic for allergic severe asthma, with fewer exacerbations, less oral corticosteroid use, and better quality of life in patients with elevated IgE and allergic sensitization. The text reports a GINA recommendation to use omalizumab as add-on therapy from 6 years of age when perennial allergen sensitization persists despite high-dose inhaled corticosteroids and another controller. Anti-IL-5 and anti-IL-5-receptor therapies are described as established add-on maintenance treatment for severe eosinophilic asthma in adults and children. Treatment response should be assessed after at least 4 months.
