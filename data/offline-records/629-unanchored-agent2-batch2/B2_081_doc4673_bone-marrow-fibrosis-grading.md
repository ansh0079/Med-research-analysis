## Synopsis (gist)

This European consensus grades bone marrow fibrosis in four steps, scored only in haematopoietic areas. MF-0 is scattered linear reticulin without intersections, matching normal marrow. MF-1 is a loose reticulin network with many intersections, especially around vessels. MF-2 is a diffuse dense increase in reticulin with extensive intersections and only focal collagen or osteosclerosis, and MF-3 adds coarse collagen bundles, often with significant osteosclerosis. The stain is checked against normal vessel-wall staining, lymphoid nodules, vessels, and fibres around fat cells are left out of the score, and normal reticulin is called grade 0 rather than grade 1.
