# Sedation and analgesia in mechanically ventilated patients: ABCDEF bundle: evidence record (629 unanchored batch 2, not published)

**Lead document:** Association of nurse-led targeted sedation-analgesia management with ventilation duration and sedation quality in mechanically ventilated patients (stored document id 5114, PMID 42430391). Read from the stored text.

**Fit:** partly

## Synopsis (gist)

This retrospective cohort studied nurse-led targeted sedation-analgesia management in mechanically ventilated adults in an anesthesia intensive care unit. Nurse-led management was associated with higher sedation target attainment, lower exposure to deep sedation and agitation, shorter duration of invasive mechanical ventilation, and shorter ICU length of stay. No significant differences were observed in unplanned extubation, reintubation within 48 hours, tracheostomy, or 28-day mortality, and delirium showed a nonsignificant downward trend. The authors state that these findings should be interpreted as hypothesis-generating associations rather than causal evidence of effectiveness because of the retrospective design, small sample size, limited adjustment, and residual confounding. The discussion cites meta-analytic evidence that ABCDE/ABCDEF bundles are associated with improved delirium-related outcomes, while noting substantial heterogeneity, and cites syntheses suggesting that protocolized sedation may reduce ventilator days, ICU length of stay, and certain safety events.
