## Synopsis (gist)

Despite guideline recommendations in MINOCA, diagnostic and management approaches vary. UK clinicians demonstrate considerable variation in preferences for investigation of patients with MINOCA, with clinical presentation strongly influencing CMR utilisation. The extent of variation in investigation preferences and the low endorsement of invasive coronary function testing highlight the absence of a standardised diagnostic approach. UK clinicians demonstrate variation in the investigation of patients with MINOCA, with the clinical presentation influencing whether to perform further investigations despite diagnostic uncertainty. There was a lack of endorsement of invasive coronary vasomotor function testing and limited uptake of intravascular imaging across the UK in the provided scenarios. These findings support the development of standardised MINOCA care pathways, enhanced and equitable diagnostic access and educational initiatives to address the observed variation in clinical practice.
