## Synopsis (gist)

This nationwide cohort examined whether cardiac resynchronization therapy is associated with optimization of guideline-directed medical therapy in HFrEF. In the abstract, CRT implantation was associated with enabled use and dose of heart failure GDMT and with decreased loop diuretic need. In the conclusion, CRT implantation was associated with optimization of beta-blockers, ACEi/ARB/ARNi, and MRA, with a stronger association for beta-blockers. CRT implantation was also linked with lower loop diuretic need, which may preserve renal function and blood pressure. The discussion states that a significant increase in systolic blood pressure in patients with advanced HFrEF receiving CRT, compared with a medication group, was described in the COMPANION trial.
