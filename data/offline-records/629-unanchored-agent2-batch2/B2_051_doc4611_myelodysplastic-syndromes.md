## Synopsis (gist)

This phase III trial compared azacitidine with conventional care in higher-risk myelodysplastic syndromes, with conventional care selected before randomisation as best supportive care, low-dose cytarabine, or intensive chemotherapy. Higher-risk disease meant an international prognostic scoring system rating of intermediate-2 or high. Median overall survival was 24.5 months with azacitidine versus 15.0 months with conventional care. At 2 years, 50.8% of the azacitidine group were alive compared with 26.2% of the conventional-care group. The authors conclude that azacitidine increases overall survival relative to conventional care. Peripheral cytopenias were the most common grade 3 to 4 adverse events for all treatments.
