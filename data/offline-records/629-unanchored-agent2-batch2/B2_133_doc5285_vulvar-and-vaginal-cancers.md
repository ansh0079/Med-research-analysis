## Synopsis (gist)

The 2023 ESGO update gives guidance on diagnosis and treatment of vulvar cancer, including referral, staging, pathology, surgery, radiotherapy, systemic treatment, recurrence, and follow-up. Planning of staging and treatment should be made on a multidisciplinary basis, patients should be counseled about options, and supportive care and psychological support should be offered throughout the pathway [V, B]. Suspicious inguinofemoral nodes should be assessed by ultrasound-guided fine-needle aspiration or core needle biopsy if this would alter primary treatment [III, A], and MRI is recommended when the invasive tumor clinically involves surrounding tissues or the finding is equivocal [IV, B]. Post-operative radiotherapy to the vulva is indicated when invasive disease extends to the pathological excision margins and further excision is not feasible [IV, B]. In locally advanced vulvar cancer, definitive capecitabine-based or cisplatin-based chemotherapy should be considered as an alternative to extensive surgery, and (chemo)radiotherapy is recommended for pelvic lymph node recurrence with or without inguinofemoral recurrence [V, B].
