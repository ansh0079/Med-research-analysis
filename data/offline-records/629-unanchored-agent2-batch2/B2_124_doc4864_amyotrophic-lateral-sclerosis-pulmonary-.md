## Synopsis (gist)

The PACTALS guidelines apply GRADE methods to ALS management in the Asia–Pacific region, and Section 4 addresses respiratory symptoms, a common cause of death from weakness of the diaphragm and other ventilatory muscles. The panel states a strong recommendation, with high quality of evidence, to offer non-invasive ventilation for ALS patients with symptoms, signs, or investigations that support respiratory insufficiency, and notes that the ability to offer NIV depends on local service availability. Early institution of NIV may be considered depending on FVC values, respiratory muscle strength training cannot be recommended at this stage, and diaphragmatic pacing is not recommended. Where breathlessness is refractory to NIV, the guideline says to consider palliative non-pharmacological strategies and opioids, and the strength of recommendation for palliative pharmacological measures is moderate with moderate quality of evidence. Cough augmentation techniques should be offered to ALS patients with weak cough, manual assisted cough and unassisted breath stacking should be offered, and mechanical cough devices and suction should be considered for refractory weak cough. Invasive ventilation practices vary across PACTALS countries, and expectations and tracheostomy should be discussed with patients and caregivers as part of advanced care planning.
