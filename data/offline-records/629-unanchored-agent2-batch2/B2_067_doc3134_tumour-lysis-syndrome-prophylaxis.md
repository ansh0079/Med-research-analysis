## Synopsis (gist)

The stored document is the British Committee for Standards in Haematology guideline on tumour lysis syndrome in adults and children with haematological malignancies. Intermediate-risk patients should be offered up to 7 days of allopurinol prophylaxis along with increased hydration. In high-risk adults the text says TLS can be prevented in the majority using a single fixed dose of 3 mg rasburicase, and a separate contiguous clause reads buricase along with increased hydration (Grade 1B). Where rasburicase is used, the addition of allopurinol is unnecessary and has the potential to reduce its effectiveness. Potassium must not be added to the hydration fluid, and a fluid volume of 3 l/24 h is described as a reasonable adult target.
