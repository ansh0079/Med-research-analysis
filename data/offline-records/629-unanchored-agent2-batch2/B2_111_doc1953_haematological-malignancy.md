## Synopsis (gist)

Patients with hematological malignancies have a high risk of invasive pneumococcal disease because of the underlying cancer and immunosuppressive anticancer treatment. The experts unanimously agreed that pneumococcal vaccinations are important for patients with hematological malignancies, and they recommended vaccination with PCV first followed by PPSV23 8 weeks later. PCV is described as beneficial and can be strongly recommended in patients with CLL, multiple myeloma, and patients undergoing hematopoietic stem cell transplantation. In patients with CLL, the experts recommended administering PCV13 as soon as possible following diagnosis or at least 2 weeks before chemotherapy. PCV13 should be administered to pediatric patients with ALL 6 months after completion of chemotherapy. The evidence is described as insufficient to consistently recommend pneumococcal vaccination to all patients with lymphoma, AML, and adults with ALL, so a case-by-case decision is necessary.
