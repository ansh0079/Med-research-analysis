## Synopsis (gist)

In the 0–6 hour time window, the guideline recommends mechanical thrombectomy plus best medical management, including intravenous thrombolysis whenever indicated, over best medical management alone for large-vessel-occlusion anterior circulation stroke without an extensive infarct core, for example ASPECTS at least 6 or an infarct core volume of 70 mL or less. In the 6–24 hour time window, it recommends mechanical thrombectomy plus best medical management over best medical management alone when patients fulfil DEFUSE-3 or DAWN selection criteria, including estimated infarct core volume. Quality of evidence for that 6–24 hour recommendation is described as moderate and the strength of recommendation as strong. Patients with an extensive infarct core, for example ASPECTS below 6 or core volume above 70 mL or above 100 mL, are recommended for inclusion in randomized trials of mechanical thrombectomy plus best medical management versus best medical management alone. If a dedicated trial is not possible, the experts suggest that mechanical thrombectomy may be reasonable in selected cases with ASPECTS below 6 or core volume above 70 mL.
