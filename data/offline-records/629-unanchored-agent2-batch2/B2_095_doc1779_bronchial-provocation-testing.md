## Synopsis (gist)

This ERS technical standard, endorsed by the American Thoracic Society, replaces a methacholine concentration result (PC20) with the delivered dose that causes a 20% fall in FEV1 (PD20). Any nebuliser or dosimeter may be used if its output and particle size are known. Inhalation is by tidal breathing for at least 1 minute, or by a calculated dosimeter breath count, and a deep-breath method is not recommended because a deep breath protects against constriction. The protocol starts at a delivered dose of 1 to 3 micrograms and steps up by doubling or quadrupling until FEV1 falls by 20% or a delivered dose of 400 micrograms is reached. Clinical testing uses quadrupling steps, doubling steps are preferred when the test is used to measure change after treatment, and a diluent step is supported.
