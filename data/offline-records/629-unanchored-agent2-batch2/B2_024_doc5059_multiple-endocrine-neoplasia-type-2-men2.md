## Synopsis (gist)

This review addresses genotype-phenotype correlation in multiple endocrine neoplasia type 2, including MEN2A and MEN2B and rearranged during transfection mutations. The specific mutation may suggest a predilection toward a particular medullary thyroid carcinoma phenotype, and recommendations on the timing of prophylactic thyroidectomy are based on classifying mutations into risk levels. Thyroidectomy is recommended before 5 years of age for higher-risk ATA C mutations and as early as possible, preferably within the first year after birth, for ATA D mutations. For ATA A mutations, the source describes differing suggestions, including that surgery may be postponed until an abnormal C-cell stimulation test is obtained. Biochemical screening for pheochromocytoma should be performed annually according to genotype, and women with a rearranged during transfection mutation associated with MEN2 should be screened for pheochromocytoma before pregnancy. Genetic testing is described as standard care for first-degree relatives of patients with newly diagnosed medullary thyroid carcinoma and is also recommended for patients with medullary thyroid carcinoma.
